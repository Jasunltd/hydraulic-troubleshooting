# Attribution for data-derived hydraulic evidence

The evidence tables, reports and hashes in this folder analyze the
UCI dataset *Condition monitoring of hydraulic systems*
(dataset 447; DOI https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CW21),
by Nikolai Helwig, Eliseo Pignanelli and Andreas Schütze.

Upstream source and CC BY 4.0 license:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/447/condition+monitoring+of+hydraulic+systems
and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

**Changes:** Study-generated rankings, holdout comparisons, audits,
candidate rules and source-output hash receipts were calculated from
the original public test-rig sensor data. They must not be represented
as original UCI publications or as independently measured field
maintenance results. Preserve original-source credit and the study's
modification note when sharing data-derived reports.

Code and independently authored analysis/test logic are MIT-licensed
only where the relevant rightsholders have approved them. The root
[THIRD-PARTY-DATA.md](../../../THIRD-PARTY-DATA.md) details the evidence
classes and non-claims.
